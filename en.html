Studio

A house of sonic craft: precise, bold and culturally rooted.

I am Chadrack Maluka. Chady's Corner is an independent studio at the crossroads of sonic identity, music production and audio culture. I help brands, agencies, artists, venues and media build memorable sonic signatures, far from generic jingles.

Sonic IdentitySound logos, themes, jingles, mini sonic guidelines and variations that make a brand recognizable by ear.
Original CompositionCustom music for brand films, social content, events, title sequences, podcasts and campaigns.
Sound designTextures, impacts, transitions, atmospheres and audio dressing that give image more depth, presence and memory.
Audio Post-productionSound editing, voice cleanup, image sync, mixing, mastering and multi-format adaptations.
